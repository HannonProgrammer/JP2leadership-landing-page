Why This Track

A Degree for Executives and Professionals: M.A. "John Paul II and Leadership"

The Saint John Paul II Institute is developing a graduate initiative that builds on its existing strengths in philosophy, theology, ethics, culture, and the thought of St. John Paul II while expanding into applied leadership formation for contemporary professional life.

The proposal emerged from sustained consultation with accomplished students, University leadership, faculty, donors, and supporters who recognized that the Institute's current work is already functioning as leadership formation for serious professionals.

Leadership Formation

John Paul II as thinker, pastor, statesman, artist, and global leader.

This track presents St. John Paul II not only as a theologian and philosopher, but as a multifaceted leader whose thought remains highly relevant to modern leadership challenges.

  • Ethical and human-centered leadership
  • Organizational culture and decision-making
  • Leadership philosophy informed by faith and reason
  • Applied reflection on solidarity, culture, politics, and the arts

The degree is intended to move beyond a traditional academic model by integrating serious study, practical leadership development, and mission-driven professional formation.
