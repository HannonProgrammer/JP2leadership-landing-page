Detailed Experience

Explore teaching, mentorship, and leadership contributions.

Dr. Corrigan brings a blend of philosophical depth, pastoral wisdom, and executive education experience. He oversees curriculum development for the leadership track and works closely with students on ethical decision-making and mission-driven strategy.

A specialist in cultural formation and leadership, Dr. Kowalska designs learning experiences that connect John Paul II's thought to real-world organizational and cultural challenges.

Prof. Alvarez advises on immersive practicum design and partnership development, helping participants move from theory to trusted leadership practice.
